PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Privacy Policy.

QuietLink is designed to shorten URLs without accounts, click analytics, or a personal link dashboard. This policy explains what the service needs to work.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information stored to create links

When you create a short link, QuietLink stores the destination URL, whether a destination preview is required, an expiry timestamp if you choose an expiry, and a hash of a private management key used to revoke the link. A random short code points to that record. QuietLink does not ask for your name, email address, password, or account registration.

New links also have a private management key so the creator can revoke the link without an account. The key is shown once when the link is created; QuietLink stores only a SHA-256 hash of the key, not the key itself. If the key is lost, QuietLink cannot reveal it or issue a replacement for that link.

Destination previews and QR codes

When a creator enables destination preview, visitors see the destination domain and full URL on a QuietLink page before they choose to continue. QuietLink does not fetch the destination page to generate this preview. QR codes are generated locally in the browser from the short URL; the URL is not sent to a third-party QR generation service.

Analytics and application logs

QuietLink does not implement click counting, visitor profiles, analytics cookies, or a link-history dashboard. Its redirect code does not deliberately record visitor IP addresses, referrers, or click timestamps. The website does not load third-party analytics scripts.

Abuse prevention

To reduce automated link spam, the link-creation endpoint applies a best-effort, short-lived in-memory request throttle. It derives an ephemeral hash from Cloudflare's source-IP header and the current minute; the raw source IP and throttle state are not written to QuietLink's link database or application logs. This throttle is local to a running Worker isolate, so it is not a globally coordinated rate limit. Cloudflare still processes the network request under its own infrastructure policies.

Hosting and network processing

QuietLink is hosted using Cloudflare Pages, Pages Functions, and Workers KV. Cloudflare processes network requests and may handle technical metadata under its own policies and infrastructure settings. We cannot promise that requests are invisible to the hosting provider or that using a shortened URL makes you fully anonymous.

Cookies and browser storage

The QuietLink application does not set analytics cookies or maintain a local history of links you create. Your browser and hosting provider may still use technical mechanisms necessary to operate or secure the service.

Expiration and retention

Links may expire after a selected duration or be set to never expire. Expiring records are stored with a database time-to-live, and the redirect handler checks the expiry time before redirecting. An expired link should no longer work even if underlying storage cleanup takes longer.

“Never expire” means a link remains until it is removed or the service changes; it is not a guarantee of permanent availability.

Destination websites

When someone opens a short link, QuietLink redirects them to the destination they chose. That destination website may independently collect information about visitors under its own privacy policy. A short link does not mask your connection from the destination or serve as a proxy.

Responsible use

Do not use QuietLink to distribute phishing, malware, impersonation links, or unlawful content. We may need to modify or disable service functionality to protect the service and respond to abuse or legal requirements.

Children's privacy

QuietLink is not intended to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal or sensitive information in URLs you shorten.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision reflected on this page.
